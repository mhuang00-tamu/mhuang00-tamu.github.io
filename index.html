Welcome

A small instrument.
An immense musical world.

The violin has occupied a remarkable place in Western music for more than four centuries. It can sing with extraordinary intimacy, cut through the force of a full orchestra, or become an instrument of almost astonishing athleticism.

This guide is designed for curious listeners at every level: people encountering the violin concerto for the first time, students looking for a path into the repertoire, and longtime enthusiasts interested in the artists and histories behind familiar recordings.

How to use this guide

Listen with curiosity.

There is no single correct route through the violin repertoire. You might begin with a melody you recognize, a performer whose sound intrigues you, or a period of music you already love. Each page offers historical context without assuming specialist knowledge, and the listening recommendations provide starting points rather than definitive verdicts.
